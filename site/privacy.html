Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

What this covers

This policy covers the Solon Watch pre-launch website. The Solon Watch app will have its own policy when it opens.

What Solon Watch collects

The only personal information this site collects is the email address you enter in the waitlist form. You can read every page without giving any information.

The site does not use advertising, tracking pixels or analytics cookies. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the way any website you visit does.

How your email is used

  • To tell you when Solon Watch opens, and to send occasional updates about the launch.
  • Solon Watch does not sell, rent or share your email with anyone for their own marketing, and never shares it with campaigns, parties or political committees.
  • Your email is stored with the form service that runs the waitlist. That company stores it on Solon Watch’s behalf and may not use it for anything else.

Removing your email

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also write to hq@solonwatch.com to have your address deleted, and it will be removed within 30 days.

Children

The waitlist is for people 13 and older. Solon Watch does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. If a change affects how your email is used, waitlist members will be told by email first.
